# The prompt

Copy everything below the line, replace `<CHARACTER>` with your historical figure or fictional character, and send it to an agent that has browser control (see [README.md](./README.md#what-this-needs) for what that means).

If you don't have a character in mind, ask the agent to suggest a few first, with a sketch of what each one's feed would look like, before you fill this in.

---

Can you help me clean up my YouTube algorithm? I want you to retrain my recommendations to match what **<CHARACTER>** would watch, by actually using YouTube on my behalf with a browser tool instead of only telling me what to search.

Before you start, ask me:

1. Whether I want you to just add new persona-aligned viewing, or also actively prune my current recommendations (marking off-persona videos "Not interested," or blocking repeat-offender channels via "Don't recommend channel").
2. Whether I want watch-only engagement, or watch plus like plus subscribe: the latter is a stronger signal, but it's visible on my account.
3. Which browser you should use.

Then:

1. Break `<CHARACTER>` down into 4–6 concrete interests, based on who they are, what they'd be curious about, and the era or context they come from. Tell me the breakdown before you start so I can sanity-check it.
2. For each interest, search YouTube for solid content (lectures, documentaries, explainers, beyond the first shallow hit), open a couple of videos, and let them actually play for a meaningful chunk of real time. Watch time is the signal that matters most, more than clicks. Like what fits well, and subscribe to channels that are a strong match, according to whatever I chose above.
3. If I asked for pruning: go through my current homepage and mark videos that clearly don't fit the persona as "Not interested," or block the channel for repeat offenders.
4. When you're done, reload my homepage and tell me concretely what changed. Show me what's showing up now that wasn't before, and what's gone, rather than just saying it worked.
5. Be honest about the limits: this is one session's worth of signal. Tell me what keeps it going or lets it slide back, and offer to run another pass later if I want one.

Don't sign into any account on my behalf, and don't ask me for a password. I'll log into YouTube myself before you touch the browser.
